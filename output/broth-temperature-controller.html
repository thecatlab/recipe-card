Broth temperature controller

Parts + function

  1. 01
    Temperature probe

    DS18B20 inside a closed stainless sleeve. Verify food contact and continuous hot-immersion suitability.

  2. 02
    Pot rim clip

    Holds the tip at mid-depth, clear of the base and walls. Cable and seals stay above the liquid.

  3. 03
    Controller enclosure

    ESP32-C3, status LED and buzzer. Set the target in a local browser page. Keep the box dry and cool.

  4. 04
    Removable knob drive · Phase 2

    Positional servo, knob collar and worktop clamp. Align with the knob shaft; allow quick removal for manual access.

  5. 05
    Phone notification

    Telegram alerts over kitchen Wi-Fi and internet. The local buzzer works without internet.

Fit assumptions: single-burner countertop gas stove; existing pot and USB supply. Measure knob diameter, shaft height, usable turn range and torque before designing the mount. No stove measurements have been supplied.

01 / Hardware + software requirements

Measurement
Genuine DS18B20: chip accuracy ±1°C up to 100°C [S1]. Calibrate and verify the complete probe assembly.
Food contact
Verified closed food-contact sleeve and continuous hot-immersion rating of at least 100°C. Waterproof alone is insufficient [S2].
Example target
92°C with a ±2°C band, measured at the probe. Choose the final target for the recipe and local boiling point.
Sampling
A fresh conversion every 2 seconds; verify CRC and reject failed or stale reads. Median of 5 valid readings.
Power
Monitor: regulated USB 5 V / 1 A. Future positional servo: separate 5–6 V supply sized for stall current; never power it from 3.3 V.
Interface
Local browser page sets the target. Telegram sends notifications; LED and buzzer show status. No custom phone app is needed.

02 / Operating logic + limitations

Start
User ignites the stove and warms the broth manually, then arms the system. Automatic ignition and re-ignition are outside this concept.
Heat alerts
Outside the chosen band for 60 seconds, or a drop greater than 2°C over 5 minutes. Proposed thresholds must be tuned in pot trials.
Phase 2 control
Small knob adjustments at least 30 seconds apart. Set travel limits from the actual stable flame range; alert if it cannot hold the target.
Fault response
Probe failure alarms immediately. Phase 2 requires fail-closed gas shutdown for flame loss, low liquid, and power / controller / probe faults; no automatic restart.
Internet loss
Local sensing, control and buzzer continue. Phone delivery becomes unavailable. Gas protection must operate independently of Wi-Fi.
Gas remaining
Cooling suggests lost heat; it cannot confirm an empty cylinder, predict remaining LPG or promptly detect an extinguished flame.

04 / Prototype acceptance checks

  1. Temperature: compare the complete probe with a reference at 90–96°C. Assembly accuracy target: within ±1°C.
  2. Monitoring: run a supervised 24-hour water trial. Trigger high / low temperature, probe-disconnect and Wi-Fi-loss conditions.
  3. Actuation: first verify knob travel and removable mounting on an unlit stove. Validate independent shutdown before gas control trials.
  4. Control target: demonstrate the selected ±2°C band after settling. This is an unverified design goal, not guaranteed performance.
